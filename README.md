# 🌦️ W.A.T.C.H.

### Weather Alert Tracking and Community Hazard System

**W.A.T.C.H.** is an **AI-powered weather and safety information system** designed to help students and community members stay informed about weather conditions, potential hazards, and important announcements based on their location.

The system combines weather updates, hazard detection, official announcements, school suspension alerts, AI-generated safety tips, and location-based notifications into one platform.

## 🎯 Purpose

The purpose of W.A.T.C.H. is to provide users with timely and relevant information that can help them stay informed and prepared during severe weather and emergency situations.

## ✨ Features

* 🌤️ **Weather Monitoring** — Provides weather information based on the user's location.
* ⚠️ **Hazard Detection** — Identifies possible hazards and risks that may affect the community.
* 📢 **Official Announcements** — Monitors official social media pages and announcements for important updates.
* 🏫 **School Suspension Alerts** — Provides notifications regarding school suspensions.
* 🤖 **AI-Generated Safety Tips** — Provides safety recommendations based on weather and hazard conditions.
* 📍 **Location-Based Notifications** — Sends relevant alerts based on the user's location.

## 👥 Target Users

W.A.T.C.H. is designed primarily for:

* Students
* Community members
* Schools and educational institutions
* System administrators

## 🚧 Project Status

**W.A.T.C.H. is currently under development.**

This repository contains the ongoing development of the system, including its features, components, and documentation.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

W.A.T.C.H. is an academic project designed to assist users in accessing weather and safety information. It should not replace official warnings, announcements, or instructions from government agencies and emergency authorities.
